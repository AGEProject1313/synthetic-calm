Public Funds

The Water That Never Arrived

January 19, 2022 · By Giulia Verra

Public funds allocated to rural water infrastructure projects in Santa Cruz Province.

The Beacon archive image. File reference: water-project.png

In several rural communities across Santa Cruz Province, water infrastructure exists mostly on paper.

Budgets were approved. Contractors were listed. Deadlines were announced. Yet residents still rely on tanks, private deliveries and improvised wells.

The Beacon reviewed municipal filings, procurement notes and local testimony. The record is incomplete, but the pattern is familiar: public money moving faster than public works.

No single document proves deliberate fraud. What remains is the harder question: how many failures must align before incompetence becomes policy?
