The Last Fishermen of Bahía Azul

September 8, 2022 · By Giulia Verra

Economic collapse of a small coastal fishing community.

The Beacon archive image. File reference: bahia-azul.png

Bahía Azul once moved with the rhythm of boats, nets and early morning markets. Today most docks are quiet before noon.

Local fishermen describe shrinking catches, rising fuel costs and licensing rules designed for operators far larger than themselves.

The decline is not dramatic enough to become national news. That may be why it has continued almost unnoticed.
