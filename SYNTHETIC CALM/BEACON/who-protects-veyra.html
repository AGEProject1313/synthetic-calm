Veyra Files

Who Protects Veyra?

April 3, 2025 · By Giulia Verra

Unidentified security teams operating around restricted areas.

The Beacon archive image. File reference: ghost-teams.png

For a city built on transparency, Veyra has a surprising number of locked doors.

Over the past eighteen months, The Beacon has collected testimony from residents, former contractors and maintenance personnel describing unidentified security teams around restricted infrastructure zones.

No official unit matches the descriptions provided.

Witnesses describe unmarked vehicles, dark uniforms, limited verbal contact and night operations near technical access points.

The phrase 'Ghost Teams' is not official. It is local shorthand, repeated by people who insist they saw something but cannot prove what it was.

There is no evidence that these operators have committed unlawful acts. There is also no clear answer to a basic civic question: who authorizes them?

Veyra's administration declined to comment on operational security arrangements.

Editor’s Note: The Beacon has requested clarification from the Veyra Administration. No detailed response was received before publication.
