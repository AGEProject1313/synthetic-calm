The Forgotten Tracks

November 17, 2023 · By Giulia Verra

Abandoned railway infrastructure and missing redevelopment funds.

The Beacon archive image. File reference: forgotten-tracks.png

The railway line north of Río Turbio was never officially abandoned. It was simply allowed to become unusable.

Documents reviewed by The Beacon show multiple redevelopment allocations over six years. The tracks remain broken.

Residents along the route describe the same pattern: announcements, surveys, political photographs, then silence.
