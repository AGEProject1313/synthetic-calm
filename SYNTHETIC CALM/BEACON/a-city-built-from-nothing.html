Veyra Files

A City Built From Nothing

March 2, 2023 · By Giulia Verra

First report on the announcement of the Veyra project.

The Beacon archive image. File reference: veyra-construction-site-2023.png

Veyra has been presented as a city designed from first principles: cleaner, safer and more efficient than the urban systems we inherited.

Its backers describe a controlled experiment in civic technology. A place where infrastructure, governance and data can finally operate as one.

The ambition is difficult to dismiss. So is the opacity around financing, land control and future administrative authority.

Perhaps Veyra will become the city its founders promised. The first question, however, is simpler: who gets to decide what a perfect city costs?
