Borderlands

The Silent Border

October 11, 2024 · By Giulia Verra

Smuggling routes and undocumented crossings in remote Patagonian regions.

The Beacon archive image. File reference: silent-border.png

Some borders are loud. They have fences, lights and uniforms. Others are silent: gravel roads, weathered signs and valleys no patrol can cover for long.

In these spaces, local economies often depend on what official maps prefer not to see.

The Beacon spoke with drivers, shepherds and former transport workers who describe a region where legality is less a line than a negotiation.
