Author Profile

Giulia Verra

Freelance investigative reporter contributing to The Beacon.

Verra focuses on corporate power, closed systems, civic surveillance, public infrastructure and private governance. Her work for The Beacon spans rural communities, border regions, public funds and the long shadow of the Veyra project.

She is not a staff employee. She files as a freelance contributor and is paid per piece.
